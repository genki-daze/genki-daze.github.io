Antibody language models can not only identify existing sequences but also generate new ones. I am
working on generating sequences from antigen information supplied as a prompt, and on evaluating how
well those sequences reflect the response to the target antigen — with antibody design and immune
response analysis as the longer-term aim.

This work has been presented as oral talks at the IPSJ SIG Bioinformatics meetings (SIGBIO):
evaluating immune responses using antigen-prompted generated sequences (84th meeting, March 2026),
and developing a sequence-conditioned antibody generative model that acquires antigen specificity
(85th meeting, July 2026).
