I am Genki Masuda, a second-year master's student in the Department of Computer Science, School of
Computing, Institute of Science Tokyo. I am a member of the Ohue Laboratory,
where I work on antibody sequence data at the intersection of bioinformatics and machine learning.

My main topic is identifying antigen-specific antibody sequences from B cell receptor (BCR)
repertoires. I proposed **LM-QASAS**, a reference-free framework built on antibody language models,
and showed that it enriches spike-related sequences in individuals vaccinated with SARS-CoV-2 mRNA
vaccines without consulting any known antibody database. More recently I have been working on models that generate antibody
sequences themselves, conditioned on the target antigen.
